Guide

First steps
  • Account. Create one with email and password (or sign in; "Forgot your password?" sends a link). Then pick a username: 3 to 20 letters, numbers, dots or underscores. It is how friends find you; you can change it once a week. Your log syncs to the account, so any device shows the same thing. Offline still works; a red dot on Profile means changes are waiting to upload.
  • Install it. Add the app to your phone's home screen. Push notifications only work from there.
  • Four tabs. Home: today's routine, your plan and the week. Feed: your workouts and your friends'. Workout: your routines. Profile: charts, stats, friends, settings.
  • 1. Exercises. Workout › Exercises › + New exercise. Needed: a name (unique) and the target muscle. Optional: short code (up to 7 letters, shown on the strip while training), cue (one reminder under the exercise), weight step (what the +/− keys add, default 1 kg). Unilateral = one side at a time, every set is a left and a right row. A shoulder exercise also needs a head (front, side or rear), because Stats count heads separately. To delete several at once, open ⋮ on the list, choose Delete exercises, tick the ones to remove and press Delete; they leave your routines too, past workouts keep them, and Undo brings them back.
  • 2. A routine. Workout › + New routine. Name it, add exercises, set the number of sets and a rep range like 8-12 for each (unilateral: which side starts). Arrows, or the arrows button by the name, reorder. Save from ⋮. The import button at the top of the Workout tab brings in a starter pack (SBL or Bro Lifter): all of its exercises, and the routines you tick. An exercise you already have by name is not added twice; a routine that is already there is skipped, and one with the same name but other exercises comes in as "Name 2".
  • 3. Train. Start routine on its card, or Start empty workout and add exercises as you go.
Gestures
  • Pull down. On Home, Feed, Workout or Profile, drag the page down from the top and let go: the app reloads and picks up a new version, without closing it.
  • Swipe back. Start at the very left edge of the screen and swipe right to go back.
  • Hold the sun / moon. On Profile a tap switches light and dark; holding it steps through the other themes (Cocoa is only changed in Themes).
  • Tap a bar. On a chart a tap on a bar shows its value; a bar with a PR opens that workout from the bubble.
  • Swipe the weekly recap sideways to change the week.
  • Also: hold the thumb under a session to see who liked it, hold backspace on the number pad to clear a box, tap a medal for the old record, swipe a set left to delete it.
Training
  • Moving around. The strip on top jumps to any exercise (a tick = all sets done); Prev / Next walk through them; the end card is after the last one. Tap the exercise name to open its history and records.
  • Logging. Tap Weight or Reps and the number pad opens. +/− add the exercise's step; the plate keys add 2.3 kg per plate (1 or 2); holding backspace clears the box. While the pad is open Prev / Next walk weight, reps, RIR. RIR (reps in reserve) is how many reps you had left; F is failure.
  • Last time. Grey numbers in the boxes are what you did last time; the small +/− in a box is the change since then. Tap the set number to fill in last time's numbers (or the set above, if the exercise is new). When the set is full, tap it again to tick it done. Rubbing a number out un-ticks it.
  • Sets. Add Set under the list. Swipe a set left, then Delete (unilateral: both sides go).
  • Medals. A ticked set that beats your heaviest weight, estimated 1RM or best set (weight x reps) gets a medal and a short banner. Tap the medal to see the old record. The first time you do an exercise only sets the bar; nothing to beat yet.
  • Notes. The note box under the cue is for this exercise today. Next time it shows as "Last time"; the plus button writes a new one. A red warning square appears when the exercise trains a muscle you listed as injured.
  • Exercise menu (⋮ by the name). Edit exercise; Edit exercise metadata (sets and rep range, saved to your routine too); Swap (same muscle, this workout only); Skip (greyed out, left out of every number and record, arrows jump over it, Unskip undoes it); Remove from workout.
  • Top menu (⋮). Add exercise, Reorder exercises, Discard workout. The back arrow only leaves the screen: a bar at the bottom of the app shows the workout running (tap to return, x to discard). Dragging in from the left edge also goes back.
  • Timer (footer). Starts a manual rest countdown with a ring; it keeps counting if you leave and can push you when it ends. If you turned on Music, Spotify / SoundCloud buttons sit beside it.
  • Finishing. After the last exercise: reorder this workout only, add one more, name it, add a session note, End workout (it warns if sets are not ticked). Only sets with reps are kept. The summary shows records, lets you fix the start time and duration, and Share image makes a picture of it.
Routines
  • A card shows exercises, sets and the usual duration (average of the last five runs; tap it to see them). Tap the card to open the routine: a chart of every run (Volume / Reps / Time, line or bars, range), its exercises, and "View muscles targeted" (a body map).
  • ⋮ on that screen: Edit routine, Duplicate routine. Editing shows a bar at the bottom: Save or the x to discard. Reorder on the Workout tab drags cards into order. In the editor, ⋮ also has Delete (finished workouts stay).
  • Enable following (editor ⋮): friends can follow this routine. Your changes reach them; nothing they do reaches yours. "Shared" on the card shows who follows it.
  • Following a friend's routine. On their profile open a routine: Follow routine adds it to yours and keeps it in step with theirs (you can run it, and change how you log it: standard or unilateral, side, weight step, cue). Copy routine copies it once. If they stop sharing, it freezes. ⋮ › Make it mine cuts the link so you can edit; Unfollow removes it.
Exercises list and page
  • Search by name or muscle. When picking for a routine or workout, switch between Recently used and By muscle.
  • An exercise's page: records (Heaviest, Est. 1RM, Best set) with the day each was set above it (tap the date to see how many days ago), a chart of them over time with PR cups (tap a point for the workout), and every time you did it. ⋮: Edit; Reset records (older sessions stay in the history but stop counting; choose where the count restarts; Undo reset brings it back); Friends leaderboard ranks the heaviest lifts.
  • Delete an exercise from its edit screen; it leaves your routines too, past workouts keep it, and Undo is offered.
Home
  • Today. The top card shows the routine planned for today with a Start button; Pick another routine starts a different one. On a rest day, or once you have trained, it shows what is next, and Train anyway starts something else. While a workout is running it says so and takes you back to it.
  • Plan. Seven rows, Monday first, each with a routine or a rest day. Tap a day to choose its routine; a tick means you trained that day. It syncs with your account.
  • Week bar: workouts and sets so far this week (against your targets), and the week streak: weeks in a row with a workout; an empty current week does not break it.
  • Sets this week: the muscles furthest from their weekly set goal, with how many days are left. Goals are set in Weekly targets.
  • Month. Days you trained are filled (tap one to open it), planned days are dotted, a planned day earlier this week that you skipped is struck out.
  • Routines: the four that waited longest, with when you last ran them and their planned days; tap one to open it.
  • Cards on top: a friend training right now (tap to watch their sets live), the install and backup reminders, the Sunday recap (from 20:00), last month's report. Each can be closed.
  • Magnifier and bell. The magnifier finds people. The bell lists likes, comments, replies, threads you joined and new followers; tap one to open it, x or Read all to clear.
Feed
  • Feed: your workouts and your friends'; All / Me / Friends appears once friends have trained. 30 cards at a time, Load more for the rest.
  • A workout. Hold one of your cards to get the ⋮ menu below without opening it. Tap a card to open it: totals with the change versus your last run of that routine, muscle split, every set (tap a medal for the old record; a plate icon means plates were used). ⋮: Edit workout (name, start, duration, notes, weights, reps, RIR, plates, add/remove/reorder sets and exercises; Save or x at the bottom), Save as routine, Delete (Undo offered).
  • Like and comment. Thumb to like (hold it to see who liked). Bubble opens comments: write, Reply, Delete your own. Likes and comments made offline are sent later.
  • Update. Drag down from the top of Home, Feed, Workout, Profile or Settings to reload the newest version ("Version updated" appears after, with a What's new button that lists every change you have not opened yet; the list is kept until you open it).
Profile
  • Top icons: sun/moon (light or dark; hold it to cycle themes), brush (Themes), person (picture, username, name, bio), gear (Settings).
  • Counts of workouts, followers, following (tap a count for the list; Remove drops a follower). A highlight means someone follows you and you have not followed back.
  • Chart of Duration, Volume or Reps by day, week or month; Last month to Lifetime. Tap a bar for its value.
  • Dashboard: Exercises, Calendar, Stats, Friends. Below: all your workouts; the filter picks one routine; search (with 6 or more workouts) reads names, exercises and notes.
  • Calendar: Month, 3 months, Year, Multi-year (tap the title). Tap a day with a workout to open it. The streak sits on top.
Stats
  • Set count per muscle group: the last full week (a Sunday workout closes the week), sets and days per muscle against your goals; a goal is marked once reached. ⋮ › Weekly targets: sets and days for each muscle (shoulders per head), plus Total workouts a week (1 to 7). The week bar reads these.
  • Muscle distribution, Chart: radar of this period against the one before (7 days to a year) with workouts, duration, volume, sets.
  • Muscle distribution, Body: a body map coloured green to red by sets, week by week with the arrows; the strip shows which days you trained.
  • Injuries: name it and mark the muscles; the map shows them in red and those exercises get a warning in a workout.
  • Muscle readiness: a body map of what you trained in the last 72 hours; red is fresh work, green is recovering, gone is ready.
  • No recent records: lifts you still do but have not set a record in for 4+ weeks; tap one to open it.
  • Weekly recap: the week with duration, volume, sets, streak, muscles and records, against the week before. Arrows or swipe go back; the share button makes an image. Pushed on Sunday evening.
  • Monthly report: last full month: a metric over 12 months, workout days, muscles, records. Pushed on the 1st.
Friends
  • Find people by username and follow. When you follow each other you are friends: you see each other's workouts, routines, week chart, streak, and live workouts. Strangers see only name, bio and counts.
  • Friends leaderboard: the button at the end of the Friends list ranks you and every friend since Monday by workouts, with sets and volume under each name. Medals go to the top three. It is counted from the sessions your feed already holds.
  • Compare (on a friend's profile): the last 30 days side by side (muscle radar, workout count, time, volume) and the lifts you both have, by name. Open one for 1RM, heaviest and best set, plus a leaderboard. Lifts you share in a friend's workout open the same screen.
Settings
  • Account: Mail and Change password (a new address takes over once its link is opened), sync status, Sign out (this phone keeps no copy), Delete account (removes the account itself, so the email and nick are free again).
  • Notifications: Push only works in the installed app: on an iPhone open the site in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (a card on Home asks again every time the page is opened in Safari). Live updates shows if friends' news arrives instantly. Push notifications on or off, then one switch per kind: likes, comments, replies, threads, followers, rest over, workout left running (after 15 minutes), weekly recap, monthly report.
  • Workout, Music: a Spotify or SoundCloud playlist link adds a button to the workout footer. Links stay on this phone only.
  • Workout, Rest timer: switch on to start a rest by itself each time you tick a set; one length for normal sets, one for unilateral.
  • Data: Export backup (asks JSON, the whole thing, or CSV, one row per set for a spreadsheet, which does not count as a backup) / Import backup (asks JSON or Hevy; a JSON file is either merged, which adds what is missing and keeps what is here, or replaces everything on the device; it says how many workouts it skipped because they could not be read); Backup reminder (a card on Home after N days) and Last backup; Previous versions (one copy a day, 7 kept, restore saves the current state first); Erase everything (deletes your exercises, routines and workouts here and in the cloud; you type your email to confirm); Hevy import (CSV; warm-ups skipped, duplicates skipped, muscles guessed, so set a muscle on new exercises that need one).
  • Themes: presets Dark, Light, Cocoa; Mode Light / Dark / System; Document, Editorial, Receipt, Mellow (green) or Dark brown (Cocoa). Kept on this device.
  • Version: the number at the bottom of Settings opens what that version changed (when it has a note); the Guide button beside it opens this guide.

Live reactions

While you train, a friend can open your workout and send you a like, a flex or a short word, which pops up on your screen, and a pair of eyes shows you who is watching. Switch it off and none of that reaches you; your friends see their buttons greyed out.

Previous versions

Once a day, when you open the app, a copy of everything on this phone is saved, and the last seven are kept. If something gets lost or overwritten, open the list and restore a copy; the current state is saved first, so a restore can be undone. The copies disappear when you remove the app's icon from the home screen, so an exported backup is still the safe one.
